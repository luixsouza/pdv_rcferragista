<objective>
Fix BUG-3: the sale receipt and quote PDFs use a fixed page height (`format: [80, 250]` and `format: [80, 200]`) and silently clip the totals/payment block when there are many items. Pre-calculate the page height before instantiating jsPDF — mirroring the correct pattern in `generateCrediarioReceipt.ts:20-29` — so totals, discount and payment information always render after the items loop (PDF-01, PDF-02).

Purpose: A fast, low-risk correctness win — printed documents for large orders currently lose the TOTAL line.
Output: Dynamic-height `generateReceipt` and `generateQuotePDF` validated at ~30 / ~25 items.
</objective>
