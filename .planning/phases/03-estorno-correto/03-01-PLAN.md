<objective>
Create the pure refund-computation core for estorno and the retrocompatible type fields it needs.

Purpose: `handleRefund` in Sales.tsx currently (a) generates haver from the wrong base, (b) cancels installments with a blanket map that touches paid/cancelled, (c) restores full original quantities ignoring prior devoluções (double restock), and (d) under-reports the paid amount for mixed/split payment sales (only the crediário-paid fraction, ignoring cash entry/non-crediário paymentEntries). Centralizing the correct arithmetic in a pure, side-effect-free module (mirroring the `processReturn.ts` precedent from FND-02) makes the financial rules verifiable in isolation and keeps Sales.tsx as a thin wiring layer (Plan 02).

Output: `src/lib/processRefund.ts` (pure function) + `Sale.cashRefundOut?` and `Sale.cancelledInstallmentIds?` optional fields.
