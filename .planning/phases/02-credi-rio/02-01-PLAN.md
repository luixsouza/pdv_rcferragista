<objective>
Create the shared overdue-status foundation for Phase 2 and extend the CreditPayment type with an auditable interest field.

This plan establishes the single source of truth for "is this installment overdue?" so that the Crediário screen (Plan 02) and the POS delinquency block stay consistent, and prepares the data contract (Plan 03) for recording interest charges.

Purpose: CRED-02 requires overdue detection that does not depend on the mount-only useEffect in CreditNotes.tsx (CONCERNS.md "useEffect Overdue Detection Runs Once on Mount Only", lines 148-156). POS.tsx (lines 137-140) currently blocks delinquent crediário sales by reading the *persisted* `status === 'overdue'`, which is stale when the app stays open past a due date. A shared helper fixes both. CRED-03 (Plan 03) needs a backward-compatible place to record the interest component on the payment audit record.

Output: `src/lib/installmentStatus.ts` (new), POS.tsx wired to use it, `CreditPayment.interestAmount?` field added.
</objective>
